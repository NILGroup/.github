# Natural Interaction Based on Language

NIL Research Group @ [FDI](https://informatica.ucm.es)-[UCM](https://ucm.es)

Welcome to our GitHub repository, where we publish and manage our public
research and teaching.

## Language, stories and interaction between people and machines

NIL (Natural Interaction based on Language) is a research group at the
Universidad Complutense de Madrid. We study how computers can understand,
generate and use language, stories and interactive media, and how these
technologies can make information accessible to everyone and support learning.

### Research Lines

- Computational Narrative and Creativity
- Language Technology for Accessibility
- Interactive and immersive media
